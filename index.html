GeoAI · Urban computing

Minwei Zhao 赵敏伟

PhD candidate in Urban Governance and Design at HKUST(GZ), and a member of the Urban Morphology Studio. MSc, UCL Geography.

I study how computer vision, graph learning, and spatial analysis can represent urban morphology and inform spatial equity, public health, and sustainable cities.
